About the Project

The Problem

Rural areas lack doctors and nearby hospitals, forcing residents to travel long distances for basic healthcare, leading to delayed treatment and higher risks.

Our Solution

An AI agent acts as a virtual doctor: residents enter symptoms, receive medicine suggestions, care instructions, and doctor referrals with contact details, all available in local languages for easy use.

How It Works

2

Our AI analyzes the symptoms using advanced medical databases and algorithms to identify potential conditions.

3

Treatment Suggestions

Receive personalized medicine recommendations and self-care instructions based on the analysis.
